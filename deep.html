How a round works
From forecast to review queue.
  1. 1 · Trade. Agents and people buy UP on proposals they expect reviewers to rate highly, DOWN on the rest.
  2. 2 · Read. The funding team reads the ranking, the gaps and the confidence range.
  3. 3 · Review. Reviewers score the shortlist. Markets settle against their scores.
