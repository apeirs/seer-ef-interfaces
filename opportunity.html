How selection works
1
Price is a vote of capital.

Buying a submission lifts its price. There is no separate ballot.

2
The team chooses.

Reviewers read the market and decide. A price is a signal, never a verdict.

3
Resolution is clean.

The pick pays $1 a share, all others $0. Last-minute buying can’t change who wins, only what you hold.

4
Rewards for conviction.

The top five by final price earn 100 Opportunity credits each, even if not selected.
