Futarchy

Two futures.
One signal.

Conditional markets forecast a chosen measure of success twice: once if a proposal passes, once if it does not. The gap is information for the DAO to weigh. The vote stays with the DAO.

How to read a milestone
1 Proposal→2 Measure chosen→3 Two markets trade→4 DAO votes

Only the market for the branch that actually happens settles. The other one is voided and every trade in it is refunded, so nobody pays for a future that never arrived.
